# Lighter-Weight Competitor Analysis: Why Not QBism? Why Not CDT?

**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Status:** Addendum to 29-schisms-deepdive (addresses Red Team HIGH findings H3, H4)
**Purpose:** Schism-by-schism evaluation of QBism and Causal Dynamical Triangulations

---

## 0. The Occam's Razor Challenge

Both the QBism and CDT frameworks resolve significant subsets of the 29 schisms with dramatically less mathematical machinery. QBism resolves 4 observer schisms (S10, S25, S21, S27) with ONE conceptual move. CDT resolves 4 spacetime schisms (S1, S3, S11, S23) with ONE structure. The needle-threading framework uses 5 layers to resolve all 29.

**The challenge:** If lighter frameworks are SUFFICIENT for the schisms they address, why accept the heavier one?

**Answer strategy:** Evaluate each framework schism-by-schism against the full 29, identifying precisely which schisms each framework resolves, which it leaves unaddressed, and whether those unaddressed schisms are genuine physics problems or philosophical preferences.

---

## 1. QBism: One-Move Resolution of Observer Schisms

### 1.1 What QBism Is

Developed by Fuchs, Schack, Mermin, and others (2010-2023), QBism makes ONE move:

> **Quantum states are an agent's degrees of belief about future experiences. The wavefunction is not a physical object — it is a tool for an agent to manage expectations.**

### 1.2 What QBism Resolves (with Justification)

| Schism | QBism Resolution | Adequacy |
|--------|-----------------|----------|
| **S10: Embedded Agent** | The agent IS the theory. No "external observer" because all quantum states are an agent's beliefs. | ✅ **FULL.** QBism was BUILT for this. |
| **S25: God's-Eye View** | There is no "God's-eye state" — all states are agent-relative. The concept of an objective state vector independent of any agent is meaningless. | ✅ **FULL.** Again, QBism's core innovation. |
| **S21: Objective Physical State** | The state is always relative to an agent. "The state of the qubit" has no meaning without specifying: state for WHOM? | ✅ **FULL.** Relational QM made operational. |
| **S27: Map vs. Territory** | The wavefunction IS the map (the agent's belief). The territory is the agent's future experiences. The map-territory confusion is a category error that QBism corrects. | ✅ **FULL.** |
| **S7: Determinism vs. Probability** | Probabilities are an agent's degrees of belief. They are epistemic, not ontic. The Born rule is a normative constraint on how an agent should update beliefs given new experiences — not a physical law. | ✅ **PARTIAL.** Resolves the philosophical tension but does not DERIVE the Born rule. |
| **S26: Single vs. Many Worlds** | Many-worlds is simply the belief that all branches "exist." QBism says: a branch exists to the extent that an agent assigns it non-zero probability. The question "do all branches exist?" is ill-posed — existence is an agent-relative concept. | ⚠️ **PHILOSOPHICAL.** QBism dissolves rather than resolves — some find this unsatisfying. |

### 1.3 What QBism Does NOT Resolve

| Schism | Why QBism Fails |
|--------|----------------|
| **S1: Continuum vs. Discrete** | QBism says nothing about the mathematical substrate. It accepts standard QM's Hilbert spaces and continuous wavefunctions. |
| **S2: State vs. Process** | Standard QM's split between kinematics and dynamics is preserved in QBism. |
| **S3: Background Independence** | QBism adds nothing to GR/QFT compatibility. |
| **S6: Locality/Separability** | QBism says non-locality is about belief-updating, not physical action-at-a-distance. But this is an interpretation, not a derivation from first principles. |
| **S11: Problem of Time** | No treatment. |
| **S14: Real vs. Complex** | QBism uses standard complex QM. No engagement with number-field questions. |
| **S19: Nomological Dualism** | QBism does not address the law/state split. The Born rule and dynamical laws are still externally specified. |
| **All Layer 1 (Mathematical Substrate) schisms** | QBism is an interpretation OF QM, not a reformulation of its mathematical foundations. It accepts all the standard mathematical assumptions. |
| **All Layer 4 (Spacetime/Gravity) schisms** | QBism extends standard QM; it does not propose new geometry. |

### 1.4 QBism Scorecard

| Layer | Schisms Resolved | Schisms Total | Coverage |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|----------|
| Layer 1 (Math) | 0 | 5 | 0% |
| Layer 2 (Ontology) | 2½ (S7, S21, S26 partial) | 8 | 31% |
| Layer 3 (QC Divide) | 0 | 6 | 0% |
| Layer 4 (Spacetime) | 0 | 6 | 0% |
| Layer 5 (Epistemology) | 4 (S10, S25, S27, S29) | 4 | 100% |
| **Total** | **6½** | **29** | **22%** |

### 1.5 QBism Verdict

QBism is **supremely efficient** at what it does — its 1-move resolution of 4 Layer 5 schisms is the most parsimonious solution in all of foundations of physics. But it is a **specialized tool** for the observer problem, not a general framework. It resolves exactly the schisms it was designed to resolve and leaves everything else untouched.

**Why the needle-threading framework is still needed:** QBism leaves 22½ schisms unaddressed. It doesn't touch the mathematical substrate (Layer 1), the quantum-classical divide (Layer 3), or spacetime/gravity (Layer 4). It is a complement to the needle-threading framework, not a replacement. A unified framework must address ALL layers, not just Layer 5.

**Synthesis opportunity:** Inject QBism's agent-as-primary insight into Layer 5 of the needle-threading framework, replacing the current hand-waving about "agent-as-node" with QBism's rigorous operational approach. This would strengthen Layer 5 without adding mathematical weight.

## 2. Causal Dynamical Triangulations: One-Structure Resolution of Spacetime Schisms

### 2.1 What CDT Is

Developed by Ambjorn, Jurkiewicz, Loll, and others (2001-present), CDT makes ONE move:

> **Spacetime is a simplicial complex — a discrete lattice of 4-simplices — with a causal structure. The continuum emerges from the large-scale limit. No smooth manifold is assumed.**

### 2.2 What CDT Resolves (with Justification)

| Schism | CDT Resolution | Adequacy |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| **S1: Continuum vs. Discrete** | CDT is discrete at the Planck scale. Spacetime is literally made of simplices, not points. The continuum is a large-scale approximation. | ✅ **FULL.** This is CDT's defining feature. |
| **S3: Background Independence** | CDT does not assume a fixed background metric. Spacetime is grown from simplex gluing rules — the geometry is generated, not assumed. | ✅ **FULL.** |
| **S11: Problem of Time** | CDT uses a Wick rotation to define a time foliation. The causal structure is built into the simplex gluing rules. | ✅ **PARTIAL.** Time is foliated, not emergent. |
| **S23: Dimensionality as Fixed** | CDT naturally produces scale-dependent spectral dimension: 2D at Planck scale, 4D at large scales. This is an OUTPUT of CDT simulations, not an input. | ✅ **FULL (numerically verified).** This is CDT's strongest empirical result. |
| **S2: State vs. Process** | CDT's path integral sums over geometries. The "state at a time" is a spatial slice of the simplicial complex. | ⚠️ **PARTIAL.** Dynamics is still path-integral, not state-traversal. |
| **S4: Arrow of Time** | CDT builds in a causal structure (foliation). The arrow is assumed, not derived. | ⚠️ **PARTIAL.** |
| **S12: Reductionism vs. Emergence** | CDT has no "fundamental level" — the simplicial complex IS the fundamental level, but at large scales it looks continuous. Both levels exist. | ⚠️ **PARTIAL.** |

### 2.3 What CDT Does NOT Resolve

| Schism | Why CDT Fails |
|--------|--------------|
| **S6: Locality/Separability** | CDT is purely geometric — it says nothing about quantum correlations or entanglement. |
| **S18: Classical Logic** | CDT uses standard mathematics and logic. No engagement with the logic question. |
| **S9: Platonism vs. Formalism** | CDT's simplicial complexes are mathematical objects. Are they "discovered" or "constructed"? CDT doesn't say. |
| **S19: Nomological Dualism** | CDT still has "simplex gluing rules" (laws) separate from "initial simplicial configuration" (initial condition). |
| **All Layer 5 (Epistemology) schisms** | CDT is written from the View from Nowhere. Observers are not in the formalism. |

### 2.4 CDT Scorecard

| Layer | Schisms Resolved | Schisms Total | Coverage |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|----------|
| Layer 1 (Math) | 1 (S1) | 5 | 20% |
| Layer 2 (Ontology) | 1 (S12) | 8 | 13% |
| Layer 3 (QC Divide) | 1 (S4) | 6 | 17% |
| Layer 4 (Spacetime) | 3½ (S3, S11, S23 + S2/S4 partial) | 6 | 58% |
| Layer 5 (Epistemology) | 0 | 4 | 0% |
| **Total** | **6½** | **29** | **22%** |

### 2.5 CDT Verdict

CDT is the **most successful discrete approach to quantum gravity** in terms of numerical results. Its demonstration of scale-dependent spectral dimension (2D → 4D) is a concrete, computable result that the needle-threading framework hasn't matched. Its resolution of 4 spacetime schisms with ONE structure is elegant.

But CDT is a **gravity-only theory.** It doesn't address the observer problem, quantum logic, the number-field question, or the mathematical substrate. It resolves exactly the schisms in Layer 4 and is silent on everything else.

**Why the needle-threading framework is still needed:** CDT leaves 22½ schisms unaddressed. It's a gravity framework, not a foundations-of-physics framework. The needle-threading framework's Layers 0-2 and 5 address schisms that CDT doesn't touch.

**Synthesis opportunity:** The CDT numerical results (spectral dimension 2D → 4D, emergence of 4D spacetime) provide INDEPENDENT SUPPORT for the needle-threading framework's claim that dimensionality is emergent (S23). The Bruhat-Tits tree's spectral dimension should match CDT's 2D → 4D flow. This is a cross-framework consistency check — if CDT and the Bruhat-Tits tree give different spectral dimensions, one (or both) is wrong.

## 4. Why Not Just Combine QBism + CDT?

### 4.1 The Incompatibility Problem

QBism and CDT cannot simply be "combined." They use different mathematical languages and make incompatible assumptions:

| Aspect | QBism | CDT |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Mathematical language | Hilbert spaces, C*-algebras | Simplicial complexes, Regge calculus |
| Spacetime | Not addressed | 4D simplicial manifold (emergent) |
| Observer | Central, not in formalism | Absent from formalism |
| Number of worlds | Indeterminate ("agent's beliefs") | Indeterminate (path integral sums over all) |
| Logic | Born rule as normative | Standard probability theory |

### 4.2 The Missing Bridge

The bridge between QBism (agent-centered quantum mechanics) and CDT (discrete emergent spacetime) is MISSING. There is no framework that:
1. Derives CDT's simplicial geometry from agent interactions
2. Derives QBism's belief-updating from discrete spacetime structure
3. Explains why both frameworks work in their respective domains
4. Predicts where they will conflict

The needle-threading framework proposes to BE this bridge:
- Layer 0-1: The tree is discrete like CDT's simplicial complex
- Layer 2: Token states are agent-relative like QBism's beliefs
- Layer 3: The Bootstrap Conjecture derives the bridge itself
- Layer 4-5: The classical continuum and observer emerge as projections

Whether it succeeds is an open question. But the GAP between QBism and CDT is real, and it's precisely the gap the needle-threading framework attempts to fill.

## 5. Falsification: When Would Lighter Alternatives Win?

The needle-threading framework becomes unnecessary (but not false) if:

| Condition | Consequence |
|-----------|------------|
| QBism + standard QM on a fixed background is shown to completely resolve S6 (non-locality), S5 (Heisenberg cut), and S7 (Born rule derivation) | Needle-threading Layers 0-2 add no value |
| CDT + an external observer model is shown to produce emergent quantum dynamics (not just emergent spacetime) | Needle-threading Layers 2-3, 5 add no value |
| The 14 "remaining" schisms (S14, S18, S9, S28, S19, S16, S13, S20, S22, S5, S6, S17, S24, S15) are shown to be philosophical, not physical | The needle-threading framework does nothing that philosophy couldn't do |

**None of these conditions currently hold.**

## 6. Conclusion

QBism and CDT are not competitors to the needle-threading framework. They are **complementary partial solutions** — QBism for the observer, CDT for spacetime. The needle-threading framework's claim to necessity rests on the remaining schisms that NEITHER resolves (the mathematical substrate, the state/law ontology, the quantum-classical bridge). 

The framework is heavier because it addresses a larger problem. The question is not "why is it heavier than QBism?" but "does it successfully address what QBism and CDT leave unaddressed?" That question remains open pending the Bootstrap Conjecture's formal proof.

The Occam's razor counterargument fails: we are not comparing two solutions to the SAME problem. QBism solves the observer problem. CDT solves the quantum gravity problem. The needle-threading framework attempts to solve the META-problem: why do these partial solutions work in their respective domains, and what is the unified structure from which they both emerge?
