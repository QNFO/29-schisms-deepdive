## 4. Constructor Theory: The Critical Challenge to S19

### 4.1 What Constructor Theory Is

Developed by David Deutsch and Chiara Marletto (2014-present), Constructor Theory makes ONE move:

> **The laws of physics are specifications of which transformations of physical systems are POSSIBLE and which are IMPOSSIBLE. A "constructor" is a device capable of causing a transformation while retaining the ability to cause it again. No dynamical equations, no initial conditions, no time-dependent state evolution.**

Key papers:
- Deutsch & Marletto, "Constructor Theory of Information," Proc. R. Soc. A 471 (2015), arXiv:1405.5563
- Deutsch & Marletto, "Constructor Theory of Time," arXiv:2505.08692 (2025)
- Marletto, Deutsch & Vedral, "Tests of Constructor Theory," arXiv:2606.07352 (2026)
- Marletto, "Constructor Theory of Thermodynamics," arXiv:1508.04129
- Marletto, "Constructor Theory of Probability," Proc. R. Soc. A 472 (2016)
- Marletto, "Constructor Theory of Life," J. R. Soc. Interface 12 (2015)

### 4.2 Constructor Theory on S19: Nomological Dualism

Constructor Theory is the **most serious external challenge** to the needle-threading framework's raison d'être because it directly targets S19 using a fundamentally different and LIGHTER formalism.

**The Constructor Theory move on S19:** Nomological dualism (the law/state split) dissolves when you reformulate physics in terms of counterfactuals about possible/impossible transformations rather than dynamical laws acting on states.

| Aspect | Needle-Threading | Constructor Theory |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Primitive** | Token tree + ultrametric | Possible/impossible tasks |
| **Laws** | Emerge from Bootstrap fixed point | ARE the counterfactual specifications themselves |
| **States** | Token configurations at tree nodes | Not primitive — described in terms of task attributes |
| **Mechanism** | Self-referential calibration (C mapping) | Counterfactual closure of the set of possible tasks |
| **Weight** | 5-layer dependency stack | Single counterfactual move |
| **Status** | Conjecture (Bootstrap unproven) | No dynamical predictions yet, but conceptual framework is well-developed |

**The challenge:** If Constructor Theory can resolve S19 WITHOUT the 5-layer apparatus, the needle-threading framework is over-engineered. The Bootstrap Conjecture becomes a heavyweight solution to a problem that admits a lightweight one.

### 4.3 What Constructor Theory Resolves (Schism-by-Schism)

| Schism | Constructor Theory Resolution | Adequacy |
|--------|------------------------------|----------|
| **S19: Nomological Dualism** | The law/state distinction does not arise. "Laws" ARE the set of possible/impossible tasks. No separate "state space" — a physical system IS defined by the set of tasks it can perform. | ✅ **FULL and LIGHTER.** This is Constructor Theory's CORE innovation. It achieves what the needle-threading framework requires 5 layers to do, with ONE conceptual move. |
| **S16: Lawfulness Assumption** | "Laws" are not fundamental entities — they are the counterfactual statements that are invariant under constructor transformations. There is no assumption of "lawfulness" beyond the invariance. | ✅ **FULL.** |

A task that is "possible with probability p" means it's possible to construct a device that produces the target state in proportion p of trials. | ✅ **PARTIAL.** Gives probability an operational meaning but does not DERIVE Born rule from counterfactuals. |
| **S21: Objective Physical State** | No "state" exists independently of the tasks that define the system. A "quantum state" is a specification of the set of tasks a system can perform (or be subject to). | ✅ **FULL.** |

No separate "kinematics" vs. "dynamics." | ⚠️ **PARTIAL.** Elegant in principle but the full formal machinery for replacing dynamical equations with counterfactuals is still in development. |

### 4.4 What Constructor Theory Does NOT Resolve

| Schism | Why Constructor Theory Fails |
|--------|----------------------------|
| **S1: Continuum vs. Discrete** | Constructor Theory is substrate-independent by design. It's agnostic about whether spacetime is continuous or discrete — it only cares about what tasks are possible ON whatever substrate exists. This is philosophically elegant but leaves the question open. |
| **S3: Background Independence** | No treatment of gravity. Constructor Theory could in principle be applied to quantum gravity (the "constructor theory of spacetime" is an active research direction) but no concrete results exist. |
| **S11: Problem of Time** | "Constructor Theory of Time" (Deutsch & Marletto, 2025) proposes that time is the resource whose availability determines which tasks are possible. This is promising but NOT yet connected to GR or the Wheeler-DeWitt equation. The paper is 150 pages of conceptual framework, not a solved theory. |
| **S6: Locality/Separability** | Constructor Theory's information-theoretic approach to entanglement is elegant but does not DERIVE locality from first principles. Non-locality is expressed as a constraint on constructor tasks, not derived from geometry. |
| **S14: Real vs. Complex** | No treatment of number fields. Constructor Theory is formulated in terms of possible/impossible tasks, which is neutral about the mathematical representation. |
| **S23: Dimensionality** | No prediction. Agnostic. |
| **All Layer 1 schisms (S9, S28, S18)** | Constructor Theory is an operational framework, not a mathematical-foundations one. It doesn't address the nature of mathematics itself. |
| **All Layer 4 schisms (S8, S24, S4, S22)** | No treatment of cosmology or gravity. |

### 4.6 Constructor Theory Verdict

Constructor Theory is the **most philosophically elegant alternative to the needle-threading framework** — and the most dangerous to its central claim. It resolves S19 (nomological dualism) with ONE conceptual move where the needle-threading framework requires 5 layers and an unproven conjecture.

**But it is NOT a replacement.** Constructor Theory resolves ~4.5/29 schisms — roughly half of QBism/CDT's coverage. It leaves ALL Layer 1 (mathematical substrate), Layer 4 (spacetime/gravity), and Layer 5 (epistemology) schisms untouched. It is an operational framework, not a mathematical-foundations one.

**Why the needle-threading framework is still needed:** Constructor Theory answers "what is a law?" but does not answer "what is the mathematical substrate from which laws emerge?" or "what is the geometric structure of spacetime?" or "what does it mean for an agent to be embedded in the system?" These are the questions the needle-threading framework's Layers 0-2 and 4-5 attempt to answer.

**Critical honesty re: S19:** If the Bootstrap Conjecture is NOT proven, and Constructor Theory's counterfactual approach to nomological dualism gains traction, the needle-threading framework's heaviest-weight argument (S19 requires self-referential calibration) would be weakened — though not falsified, since the framework would still uniquely address all 29 schisms. The framework's claim to NECESSITY (not just sufficiency) on S19 specifically is challenged by Constructor Theory.

**Synthesis opportunity:** Constructor Theory's counterfactual semantics could re-interpret the Bootstrap fixed point: T* is the set of token configurations for which "the task of distinguishing configuration A from configuration B" obeys ultrametric constraints. The Bootstrap Conjecture would then say: the only counterfactually consistent theories are those whose token-space metric is ultrametric. This is a possible HYBRID formulation that preserves the mathematical structure while gaining Constructor Theory's conceptual elegance.

## 5. Bohmian Mechanics: Deterministic Trajectories

### 5.1 What Bohmian Mechanics Is

Developed by de Broglie (1927) and Bohm (1952), revived by Dürr, Goldstein, Zanghì, and others:

> **Particles have definite trajectories guided by the wavefunction. The measurement problem dissolves because there IS a definite outcome — we just didn't know the initial particle position. Probability enters through our ignorance, not through fundamental indeterminism.**

### 5.2 Schism-by-Schism Evaluation

| Schism | Bohmian Resolution | Adequacy |
|--------|-------------------|----------|
| **S20: Unitary vs. Non-Unitary** | Bohmian mechanics is fully deterministic and unitary at the fundamental level. "Collapse" is effective — it's our updating of knowledge given the definite trajectory. | ✅ **FULL.** This is Bohmian mechanics' strongest result. |
| **S7: Determinism vs. Probability** | Determined. Probability is epistemic (ignorance of initial positions). The Born rule is derived from the "quantum equilibrium hypothesis" (|ψ|² distribution). | ✅ **FULL (with caveat).** The quantum equilibrium hypothesis is a postulate — it's not derived from the dynamics. |
| **S26: Single vs. Many Worlds** | Single world with definite trajectories. The wavefunction guides particles — there are no "other branches" that are equally real. | ✅ **FULL.** |
| **S17: Unique History** | There IS a unique history — the actual trajectory of every particle. The appearance of multiple histories is an artifact of not knowing initial conditions. | ✅ **FULL.** |
| **S6: Locality/Separability** | Bohmian mechanics is explicitly NON-LOCAL. The quantum potential depends instantaneously on the positions of ALL particles. | ⚠️ **ACCEPTS non-locality.** This is not a "resolution" — it's an acceptance that non-locality is a feature of reality. |
| **S10: Embedded Agent** | Not addressed. The observer is a physical system with a definite trajectory, but the theory doesn't develop an operational agent model. | ❌ **NOT ADDRESSED.** |
| **S21: Objective Physical State** | The wavefunction + particle positions constitute the objective state. But the wavefunction is defined on configuration space (3N-dimensional), not physical space. | ⚠️ **PARTIAL.** The ontology is clear but spacetime-unfriendly. |
| **S19: Nomological Dualism** | The wavefunction (law-like) + initial positions (initial conditions) = the law/state split is PRESERVED and made explicit. Bohmian mechanics does not resolve S19 — it EMBEDS it. | ❌ **WORSE.** The nomological dualism is explicit in the formalism. |
| **S1: Continuum vs. Discrete** | Standard continuous mathematics. No discrete substrate. | ❌ **NOT ADDRESSED.** |
| **S3: Background Independence** | Bohmian QFT and quantum gravity formulations exist but are not standard. No consensus. | ❌ **NOT ADDRESSED.** |

| Layer 5 (Epistemology) | 0 | 4 | 0% |
| **Total** | **~4** | **29** | **14%** |

### 5.4 Bohmian Verdict

Bohmian mechanics resolves the measurement problem (S20, S17, S26) elegantly but at the cost of explicit non-locality (S6) and explicit nomological dualism (S19). It is a **deterministic completion** of quantum mechanics, not a unification framework. It addresses fewer schisms than any competitor analyzed here (14%), and its explicit preservation of the law/state split makes it the POOREST fit for the 29-schisms framework — exactly the opposite direction from the needle-threading approach.

## 6. Relational Quantum Mechanics (Rovelli)

### 6.1 What Relational QM Is

Developed by Carlo Rovelli (1996-present):

> **All physical quantities are relative to an observer. There is no "absolute state of the system" — only states relative to other systems. The wavefunction collapse is not a physical process; it's the update of one observer's information about a system upon interaction.**

### 6.2 Schism-by-Schism Evaluation

| Schism | RQM Resolution | Adequacy |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| **S10: Embedded Agent** | The observer IS a physical system. Every system can be an "observer" for any other system. No privileged external perspective. | ✅ **FULL.** |
| **S25: God's-Eye View** | There is NO observer-independent description. Every description is relative to some observer. The View from Nowhere is meaningless. | ✅ **FULL.** |
| **S21: Objective Physical State** | States are always relative. "The state of S" means "the state of S relative to O." | ✅ **FULL.** |
| **S27: Map vs. Territory** | The wavefunction IS the map (one observer's information). The territory is the set of all relational facts. | ✅ **FULL.** |
| **S26: Single vs. Many Worlds** | RQM is agnostic but leans single-world: relative states are facts, not branches. | ⚠️ **PARTIAL.** |
| **S20: Unitary vs. Non-Unitary** | "Collapse" is observer-relative information update, not a physical process. Unitarity holds globally. | ⚠️ **PARTIAL.** |
| **S19: Nomological Dualism** | Not addressed. RQM changes what "states" mean but doesn't change what "laws" mean. | ❌ **NOT ADDRESSED.** |
| **S1-S6, S11-S18, S22-S24, S28-S29** | RQM is silent on mathematical foundations, spacetime, cosmology, and logic. | ❌ **NOT ADDRESSED.** |

### 6.3 RQM Scorecard

| Layer | Schisms Resolved | Schisms Total | Coverage |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|----------|
| Layer 1 (Math) | 0 | 5 | 0% |
| Layer 2 (Ontology) | 2.5 (S21, S26, S20 partial) | 8 | 31% |
| Layer 3 (QC Divide) | 0 | 6 | 0% |
| Layer 4 (Spacetime) | 0 | 6 | 0% |
| Layer 5 (Epistemology) | 4 (S10, S25, S27, S29 partial) | 4 | 100% |
| **Total** | **~6.5** | **29** | **22%** |

### 6.4 RQM Verdict

RQM is **structurally very similar to QBism** — both resolve Layer 5 by making states observer-relative. RQM's formulation is more physical (observers are physical systems, not "agents with beliefs") while QBism's is more operational (the Born rule as normative constraint on betting behavior). Both leave the mathematical substrate, quantum-classical divide, and spacetime untouched. Neither addresses S19.

RQM's score (22%) is identical to QBism's (22%). They are complementary formulations of the same insight: there is no View from Nowhere.

## 9. The Integration Problem: Why "Just Combine Them" Fails

### 9.1 Formal Incompatibilities

| Framework Pair | Incompatibility |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Constructor Theory + CDT | Constructor Theory is substrate-agnostic; CDT assumes a specific discrete substrate. How do counterfactuals about impossible transformations interact with simplex gluing rules? |
| QBism + Bohmian | QBism says states are beliefs; Bohmian says particles have definite trajectories. These are contradictory ontologies. |
| CDT + Bohmian | CDT's emergent spacetime vs. Bohmian's absolute configuration space. Incompatible geometric foundations. |
| Constructor Theory + Needle-Threading | Constructor Theory's counterfactual primitives are IN PRINCIPLE compatible with the tree formalism — the tree determines what token transformations are possible. But the Bootstrap Conjecture goes further and claims self-referential determination of the TASK SET itself. This is the tension. |

### 9.2 The Missing Bridge

The needle-threading framework proposes to be the bridge that explains WHY each partial framework works in its domain:
- Layer 0-2 explain WHY the counterfactual structure (Constructor Theory) takes the form of an ultrametric tree
- Layer 2-3 explain WHY probability (QBism/Bohmian) emerges from token valuations
- Layer 3-4 explain WHY spacetime (CDT/GUF) emerges from the Monna projection
- Layer 4-5 explain WHY observers (QBism/RQM) are embedded agents in a token world

**Whether this bridge-synthesis succeeds is the entire research program.** The frameworks analyzed here are not competitors — they are PARTIAL SOLUTIONS that the needle-threading framework claims to unify.

## 10. Falsification: When Would Alternatives Win?

| Condition | Consequence |
